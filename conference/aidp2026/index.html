About the conference

AIDP 2026 is a three-day international conference on artificial intelligence and digital pathology, held at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from 27 to 29 November 2026.

The program is built around the questions that come up once a model leaves the lab: whether it holds up across scanners, hospitals, and patient populations; how pathology images can be combined with molecular and clinical data; and what evidence clinicians and regulators need before a system is used in diagnosis.

The conference is for pathologists, computational researchers, and industry teams working on these questions. Confirmed speakers and committee members are listed below; the full program and registration details will be posted here once they are finalized.

Topics

  • Foundation models for digital pathology
  • Multimodal learning and vision–language models
  • Multi-omics integration for precision medicine
  • AI agents and automated pathology workflows
  • Clinical translation, validation, and deployment
