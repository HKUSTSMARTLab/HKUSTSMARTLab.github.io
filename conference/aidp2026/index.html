About the conference

AIDP 2026, the 2026 Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Digital Pathology, takes place at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology from 27 to 29 November 2026. Online conference registration closes on 20 November 2026.

As digital pathology advances, real-world generalizability remains a critical hurdle—an AI model that excels on one scanner often struggles on another. To address this, much of this year’s program centers on robust validation, examining whether algorithms truly hold across different clinical sites and diverse patient cohorts. Complementing this focus, dedicated sessions will explore multi-modal integration—pairing pathology images with molecular and clinical data—as well as the stringent evidentiary standards required by clinicians and regulatory bodies prior to diagnostic adoption.

Alongside oral presentations, AIDP 2026 features an interactive poster program. We actively encourage submissions of early-stage, work-in-progress findings alongside completed studies. The deadline for poster submission is 18 November 2026, with acceptance notifications issued on 20 November.

Topics

  • Foundation models for digital pathology
  • Multimodal learning and vision–language models
  • Multi-omics integration for precision medicine
  • AI agents and automated pathology workflows
  • Clinical translation, validation, and deployment
