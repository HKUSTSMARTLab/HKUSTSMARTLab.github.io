About the conference

AIDP 2026 is an international conference on artificial intelligence and digital pathology, held at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from 27 to 29 November 2026.

The conference brings together researchers, clinicians, and industry practitioners to discuss foundation models, multimodal learning, multi-omics integration, and the clinical deployment of AI in pathology. Sessions are intended to support exchange between the computational and clinical communities working on these problems.

The scientific program and registration arrangements are being finalized. Confirmed presenters and committee members are listed below; further details will be published on this site as they are confirmed.

Topics

  • Foundation models for digital pathology
  • Multimodal learning and vision–language models
  • Multi-omics integration for precision medicine
  • AI agents and automated pathology workflows
  • Clinical translation, validation, and deployment
