Pre-arrival Information

Visa guidance, local practicalities, and useful contacts for conference participants.

Welcome to Hong Kong. The information below is intended to help you prepare for your visit. Please check the official sources linked in each section, particularly for visa requirements and weather warnings.

Visa requirements

Check before you travel. Visa requirements should be confirmed on the Hong Kong Immigration Department website. Holders of passports from many countries do not require a visa for short visits, but this depends on nationality and travel document. Please allow sufficient time for any visa application.

Weather

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate with distinct seasons. Typhoon season begins in May and ends in November. When a typhoon is approaching, warnings are broadcast on television and radio.

November conditions

  • Daytime temperatureUsually around 24°C
  • Night-time temperatureUsually around 19°C
  • HumidityModerate
  • ConditionsMild and generally pleasant, with cooler evenings and occasional rain

Typhoon warnings

When typhoon signal no. 8 is in place, most businesses and shops close and flights may be cancelled. A separate warning system operates for heavy rain.

Currency and payment

Hong Kong dollar (HKD)

  • Exchange rateApproximately 7.80 HKD to 1 USD
  • Currency regimeLinked to the US dollar

Currency converter

Payment methods

  • Credit cardsAmerican Express, Visa, and MasterCard are widely accepted at hotels, shops, and restaurants.
  • Mobile paymentsApple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay, and WeChat Pay are accepted at many stores. Look for payment signs at entrances.
  • Octopus cardWidely used for public transport and shops. Physical cards are available, and a tourist app is also offered. Octopus information for visitors

ATMs

ATMs are widely available throughout Hong Kong. Many accept international cards, and HSBC machines provide 24-hour cash withdrawal for Visa and MasterCard holders. Some ATMs also offer foreign currency withdrawal.

HSBC ATM services
